FRAME 02 — How I work

Three rules I keep.

  1. 01

    Design and code in the same week

    I sketch in Figma and build in the browser side by side, so we test real interactions early and nothing gets lost in a handoff.

  2. 02

    Small scope, shipped often

    We cut the work into releases you can actually use. A working slice every two weeks beats a perfect plan that ships in six months.

  3. 03

    You talk to me, not an account manager

    The person on your calls is the person doing the work. Decisions take a message, not a meeting.
